## Current direction

Stage 4 completed with decision **Adopt**. The user-facing documentation is organised around Tutorials, How-to guides, Reference and Explanation, while project records remain under `planning/` or immutable repository history.

The modular IssueOps session initiative remains **approved**, not adopted. Slice 1 completed the ownership/decomposition decision through issue #133 and PR #134, and Slice 2 delivered the minimum Version 0.1 Reference, How-to and non-normative Example guidance through issue #135 and PR #136. Slice 3 is the current separately governed evidence boundary under issue #137; Pilot A (#138) and Pilot B (#139) gather evidence within that boundary and do not make an `Adopt`, `Adapt` or `Reject` decision. Slice 4 remains the future comparative close-out and decision boundary and has not begun.

Issue #90 remains shaping-only for a possible Stage 5 — Operational evidence assistance. Stage 4 provides a documentation architecture dependency but no Stage 5 implementation authority.

The recommended stable operating baseline remains `v0.2.0`. The proposed `v0.3.0-alpha.1` Stage 3 snapshot remains experimental and closed with decision **Adapt**.
