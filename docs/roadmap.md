## Current direction

Stage 4 completed with decision **Adopt**. The user-facing documentation is organised around Tutorials, How-to guides, Reference and Explanation, while project records remain under `planning/` or immutable repository history.

The modular IssueOps session initiative is complete with decision **Adopt after adaptation**. Slice 4 first concluded **Adapt**; bounded correction and proof through issue #143 and PR #145 resolved the material pre-adoption boundaries, after which the post-adaptation reassessment on issue #142 supported **Adopt** and issue #146 / PR #147 integrated the completed baseline. The maintained planning and delivery records above remain the authoritative source for that lineage.

Stage 5 — Operational evidence assistance — completed with decision **Adopt**. It delivered canonical non-closing execution-contract linkage, truthful read-only inline review-thread evidence and the Stage 4-aligned normal reviewer workflow, then proved the model through three qualifying ordinary implementation pull requests: #167, #173 and #176. The final owner decision is recorded on issue #90 after the completed #163 proof synthesis and fresh independent review.

The adopted Stage 5 boundary remains deliberately narrow: evidence collection is human-triggered and read-only; mechanical completeness is not approval readiness; substantive contract review, validation judgement, approval and merge authority remain human decisions. Stage 5 does not authorise automatic lifecycle transitions, collector review/approval decisions, auto-merge, repository-setting changes, permission expansion or cross-repository rollout.

Issues #141 and #144 are completed separate execution-bridge work that proved the bounded `/collect-evidence` transport used by Stage 5. The separate #148/#149 PR-diff-validation lineage also remains outside Stage 5 scope.

No later stage is currently approved. Any future stage or adaptation must begin as a separately governed planning question rather than being inferred from Stage 5 adoption.

The recommended stable operating baseline remains `v0.2.0`. The proposed `v0.3.0-alpha.1` Stage 3 snapshot remains experimental and closed with decision **Adapt**.
