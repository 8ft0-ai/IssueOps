## Current direction

Stage 4 completed with decision **Adopt**. The user-facing documentation is organised around Tutorials, How-to guides, Reference and Explanation, while project records remain under `planning/` or immutable repository history.

The modular IssueOps session initiative is complete with decision **Adopt after adaptation**. Slice 4 first concluded **Adapt**; bounded correction and proof through issue #143 and PR #145 resolved the material pre-adoption boundaries, after which the post-adaptation reassessment on issue #142 supported **Adopt** and issue #146 / PR #147 integrated the completed baseline. The maintained planning and delivery records above remain the authoritative source for that lineage.

Stage 5 — Operational evidence assistance — is now **approved**, not delivered or adopted. The approved plan keeps the existing deliberate, read-only `/collect-evidence` workflow and focuses on two minimum evidence-completeness adaptations: canonical non-closing execution-contract linkage and truthful inline review-thread evidence, or explicit unavailable/incomplete representation when the retained read-only boundary cannot provide it. Adoption remains contingent on ordinary-PR proof and a later evidence-based **Adopt**, **Adapt** or **Reject** decision.

Issues #141 and #144 are completed separate execution-bridge work that proved the bounded `/collect-evidence` transport used by the Stage 5 target workflow. That transport does not itself constitute Stage 5 adoption. The separate #148/#149 PR-diff-validation lineage also remains outside Stage 5 scope.

Stage 5 preserves human-triggered collection, read-only evidence assistance and human review/merge authority. It does not authorise automatic lifecycle transitions, review or approval decisions, auto-merge, repository-setting changes or cross-repository rollout.

The recommended stable operating baseline remains `v0.2.0`. The proposed `v0.3.0-alpha.1` Stage 3 snapshot remains experimental and closed with decision **Adapt**.
